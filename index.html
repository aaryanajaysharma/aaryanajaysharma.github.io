About Me

I am a PhD Candidate at the University of Twente, Netherlands, where I am part of the Semantics, Cybersecurity & Services (SCS) group and work on developing attacks and defences against Large Language Models (LLMs).

I completed my B.Tech. (Hons.) and MS by Research in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Hyderabad, where I was part of the SyPy Group and advised by Dr. Ankit Gangwal at the Center for Security, Theory & Algorithmic Research (CSTAR).

Previously, I was an Applied AI Researcher at ServiceNow AI Research, where I worked towards improving multi-task performance of LLMs under low resource constraints and benchmarking content moderation in agentic systems.

Research Interests

My interests lie broadly in the security and safety of machine learning systems.

  • Adversarial Machine Learning
  • Model Ownership/Watermarking
  • Explainable AI (XAI) & Graph Representation Learning
  • Security and Safety of Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • Privacy in AI
